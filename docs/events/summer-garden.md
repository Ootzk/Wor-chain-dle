# Summer Garden

**Season:** v1.7.0  
**Mode:** Event

Summer Garden is the first Wor-chain-dle seasonal Event. It uses its own answer and records, separate from Daily, and previews the cosmetics introduced for this season while you play.

> A rabbit has wandered into the peaceful Summer Garden. Collect the four-leaf clovers before it eats through the grass path!

## How It Works

After your first submitted word, the rabbit starts moving along the chain path.

- **Nibbled cells:** Letters and colors disappear from cells the rabbit has visited.
- **Stay time:** Green cells hold the rabbit for 5 seconds. Purple, gray, and fallback cells hold it for 3 seconds.
- **Game over:** If the rabbit reaches a cell in a row you have not submitted yet, the picnic is over.
- **Four-leaf clovers:** Hit clover cells in rows 2-6 with green results to collect them.
- **Win bonus:** Each win adds 1 bonus clover. An early win also collects remaining rows.

## Event Rewards

Summer Garden includes Event achievements tied to this season's play:

- Complete Summer Garden games.
- Collect 37 four-leaf clovers.
- Win after using both `GREEN` and `GRASS`.
- Win Summer Garden games within 1 minute.
- Complete the Summer Garden share badge set.

Unlocked rewards include Garden-themed share badges, share emoji, tile colors, and chain colors.

## Records

Event results are tracked separately from Daily records. Open Records in Event mode to review:

- Today's Event result
- Event calendar
- Summary and detail stats
- Clover progress
- Event achievement progress
